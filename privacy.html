SwiftTab Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Overview

SwiftTab Pro is a browser tab switcher that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Data handled locally

  • Tab titles, URLs and icons — shown in the switcher, held in memory only.
  • Page thumbnails — captured for preview, stored in session storage and cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Bookmarks — read-only, to mark bookmarked tabs with a ★.
  • Recently closed tabs — read-only, to restore the last closed tab.
  • Settings — stored with chrome.storage.sync (synced across your devices via Chrome if you have sync enabled).
  • Last 20 search queries — stored locally, clearable from the options page.

What it does not do

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No browsing history is logged.
  • No network requests are made to developer servers.
  • No cookies, trackers, analytics or advertising.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Limited use

Handled data is used only for the extension's tab-switching feature, never for advertising, and is never read by humans except with your consent or as required by law or for security.

Your control

  • Settings apply instantly from the options page.
  • Search history can be cleared with one click.
  • Thumbnails are cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all data stored by it.

概述

SwiftTab Pro 是一款完全在设备本地运行的标签页切换扩展,不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享任何个人数据。

本机处理的数据

  • 标签页标题、URL、图标 —— 显示在切换器中,仅存于内存。
  • 页面缩略图 —— 用于预览,存于会话存储,浏览器会话结束自动清空。
  • 书签 —— 只读,用于给收藏的标签打 ★。
  • 最近关闭的标签 —— 只读,用于恢复最近关闭的标签。
  • 设置 —— 存于 chrome.storage.sync(若开启 Chrome 同步,会经您的账号在您自己的设备间同步)。
  • 最近 20 条搜索记录 —— 本地存储,可在设置页清除。

不会做的事

  • 不收集个人信息。
  • 不记录浏览历史。
  • 不向开发者服务器发起任何网络请求。
  • 无 Cookie、无跟踪、无分析、无广告。
  • 不与第三方共享或出售数据。

有限使用

处理的数据仅用于扩展的标签切换功能,绝不做广告用途;除您同意或法律、安全需要外,数据不会被人工读取。

用户控制

  • 设置项在设置页即时生效。
  • 搜索历史可一键清除。
  • 缩略图随浏览器会话结束清空。
  • 卸载扩展会移除其存储的全部数据。
