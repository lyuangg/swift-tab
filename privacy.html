Overview

SwiftTab is a browser tab switcher extension. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This privacy policy explains what data the extension handles, how it is stored, and how you stay in control. The short version: SwiftTab does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

What the extension does

SwiftTab lists your open tabs and lets you switch between them quickly, using thumbnails, search and keyboard shortcuts. All of this happens locally in your browser.

chrome.storage.sync follows Chrome's standard sync behavior: if you have Chrome sync enabled, your settings may sync across your own devices through your Google account, as with any extension. Settings are never sent to the extension developer's servers.

What the extension does NOT do

  • No personal information collected. SwiftTab does not collect your name, email, or any account information.
  • No browsing history logging. It lists the tabs you currently have open, only while the switcher is shown.
  • No network requests. The extension makes no requests to any server owned by the developer.
  • No cookies, trackers, or analytics.
  • No advertising. No ads, and no use of your data for personalized or retargeted advertising.
  • No third-party sharing or sale of data.

Limited Use (Chrome Web Store compliance)

The limited data the extension handles is used solely to provide its user-facing tab-switching feature:

  • Data is used and transferred only for the single purpose of the extension.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties except where necessary for the feature, to comply with law, for security, or in the event of a merger/acquisition/sale.
  • Data is never used or transferred for personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • Data is never read by humans except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or in aggregated/anonymized internal operations.

User control & retention

  • All settings are managed on the options page and apply immediately.
  • Search history (last 20 entries) can be cleared with one click from the options page.
  • Thumbnail caches are held in session storage and are cleared automatically when the browser session ends.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all data stored by it on your device.

Security

Because data stays on your device, there is no transmission to secure. The extension requests the narrowest set of permissions needed to function and uses standard browser storage APIs that are protected by your browser and operating system.

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the extension, please open an issue or contact the developer via GitHub: github.com/lyuangg.

SwiftTab 列出您打开的标签页,借助缩略图、搜索与快捷键让您快速切换。以上全部在您的浏览器本地完成。

扩展不会做的事

  • 不收集个人信息。SwiftTab 不收集您的姓名、邮箱或任何账户信息。
  • 不记录浏览历史。它只在切换面板显示时列出您当前打开的标签。
  • 无网络请求。扩展不会向开发者拥有的任何服务器发起请求。
  • 无 Cookie、无跟踪器、无分析。
  • 无广告。不含广告,也不会将您的数据用于个性化或重定向广告。
  • 不与第三方共享或出售数据。

有限使用声明(Chrome Web Store 合规)

扩展处理的少量数据仅用于提供其面向用户的标签切换功能:

  • 数据仅用于扩展的单一用途,使用与传输范围以此为限。
  • 除功能必需、遵守法律、安全保障或发生并购/资产出售外,不向第三方传输数据。
  • 绝不用数据做个性化、重定向或兴趣广告。
  • 除您同意、安全、守法或聚合匿名化内部运营外,数据不会被人工阅读。

用户控制与保留

  • 全部设置在设置页管理,即时生效。
  • 搜索历史(最近 20 条)可在设置页一键清除。
  • 缩略图缓存存放于会话存储,浏览器会话结束自动清空。
  • 卸载扩展会移除其在您设备上存储的全部数据。

安全

由于数据留在您的设备本地,不存在传输环节需要加密。扩展只申请实现功能所需的最小权限,并使用受您浏览器与操作系统保护的浏览器标准存储 API。
